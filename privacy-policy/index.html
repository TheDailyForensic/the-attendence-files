Privacy Policy

By The Attendance Desk  |  Standing Section  |  The Daily Forensic  | 

This privacy policy explains how The Attendance Files (a special section of The Daily Forensic) handles information when you visit our pages. We are a satirical forensic-journalism desk operating out of Bangalore. We do not run a surveillance operation. We do not monetise your curiosity. We count absences, not clicks.

What We Collect

Nothing. Zero. The register is the only database we maintain, and it contains 92 school days of attendance records for one Grade 11 Science INT A class. Your IP address, browser fingerprint, scrolling depth, or the fact that you lingered on the sixteen-day block — none of it is logged by us. GitHub Pages serves static HTML. There is no backend, no analytics script, no tracking pixel, no cookie consent banner because there are no cookies to consent to.

Third-Party Requests

The page loads two external resources, both cacheable and both necessary for the design to render as intended:

  • Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Source Serif 4, Oswald) — loaded from fonts.googleapis.com. Google's privacy policy applies; we do not control it.
  • Chart.js (v4.4.1) — loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies; we do not control it.

No other third-party scripts, iframes, social widgets, or advertising networks are present.

Links to Other Properties

Our pages link to The Daily Forensic front page and to individual case reports (e.g., The Manas Situation). Each is a separate GitHub Pages site under the TheDailyForensic account. Their privacy practices are their own; this policy covers only the-attendence-files.

Data Retention

Since we collect nothing, we retain nothing. The attendance register (92 rows, 30 students) is public record in the sense that it is published in the dossier. It will not be updated after the filing date.

Your Rights

You have the right to close this tab. You have the right to forget you ever read about Parth Sarthi's Wednesdays. You have the right to demand a privacy policy that is shorter than the register itself — this one qualifies.

Contact

Questions, complaints, or formal data-subject access requests may be directed to the editorial desk via the main site's privacy page. We will respond with the same rigour we apply to attendance audits: thoroughly, skeptically, and with a sense of humour.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below will update. We do not notify users because we do not know who you are.
